Accounting Notes

  • Inventory purchases are not counted as an expense in the Profit & Loss statement.
  • The cost of stock sold is counted as Cost of Goods Sold.
  • Expenses reduce net profit.
  • Cash flow shows money moving in and out; profit and cash are not the same thing.
